Pricing FAQ

Are there setup fees?

Yes, all plans require a one-time onboarding and setup fee equivalent to 50% of the first month's retainer to cover initial audits, tracking setup, and strategy development.

Do you require long-term contracts?

We believe in earning your business every month. After an initial 90-day sprint to establish baselines, our contracts move to a flexible month-to-month model.

Is ad spend included?

No. Our retainer covers our agency's strategy, management, and creative execution. The actual advertising budget is paid directly to platforms like Google or Meta.

How do you track ROI?

We build custom, real-time Looker Studio dashboards tracking every micro and macro conversion, so you have complete transparency into your returns.
